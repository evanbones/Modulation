This module improves how walls, fences, and iron bars/panes visually connect to each other, based on [BetterWalls](https://modrinth.com/mod/betterwalls) by Lemonnik6484.

**Features:**
* **Walls Connect to Fences**: Walls visually connect to fences and wall signs.
* **Fences Connect to Walls & Bars**: Fences visually connect to walls, iron bars/glass panes, other fences, and wall signs.
* **Bars Connect to Fences**: Iron bars and glass panes visually connect to fences, fence gates, and wall signs.
