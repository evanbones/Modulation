This module contains small gameplay behavior tweaks.

**Features:**
* **Flammable Cobwebs**: Allows cobwebs to catch fire and burn.
* **Campfires Place Unlit**: Campfires are placed unlit instead of already burning.
* **Campfires Set Entities On Fire**: Campfires will set fire to entities standing on them.
* **No Dinnerlava**: Disables the generation of hidden single-block lava pockets in the Nether.
* **Ender Pearl Sound**: Plays the Enderman teleport sound when an Ender Pearl breaks upon impact.
* **Disable Ender Pearl Damage**: Removes the fall damage taken when teleporting with an Ender Pearl.
* **Spawn Egg Cracking**: Spawn eggs produce cracking particles and play a sound effect when used.
* **Disable Axe Stripping**: Prevents axes from stripping logs and wood in-world (including modded logs).
* **Disable Copper Scraping**: Prevents axes from scraping wax and oxidation off copper blocks in-world.
* **Disable Path Creation**: Prevents shovels from turning dirt and grass into dirt paths.
* **Disable Farmland Creation**: Prevents hoes from tilling dirt and grass into farmland.
* **Disable Anvil Damage**: Prevents anvils from degrading, taking damage, or breaking from use and falling.
* **Tridents In Void Return**: Loyalty tridents return when falling into the void instead of being destroyed.
* **Cactus Punching Hurts**: Breaking or punching a cactus with bare hands damages the player.
* **Chaining Creepers**: Creepers explode after taking explosion damage.
* **Furnace Minecart Accepts Any Fuel**: Allows furnace minecarts to accept any furnace fuel, rather than just coal and charcoal.
* **Infinity Bows Require No Arrows**: Bows enchanted with Infinity do not require having an arrow in your inventory to fire.
* **Tridents Accept Sharpness**: Allows tridents to accept the Sharpness enchantment on an anvil, increasing melee and thrown damage (incompatible with Impaling).
* **Bedrock-Like Impaling**: Makes the Impaling enchantment deal extra damage to any entity in water or rain.
* **Nether Cauldron**: Water evaporates into steam when placed inside a cauldron in the Nether.
* **Fire Aspect Is Flint And Steel**: Allows weapons with Fire Aspect to ignite blocks, campfires, TNT, and entities like flint and steel.
* **Cannot Open Chest When Targeted**: Prevents opening chests, barrels, and shulker boxes while being targeted by hostile monsters.
