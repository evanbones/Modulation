---
title: Passable Foliage
hide_meta: true
---

This module lets entities walk straight through leaves instead of being blocked by them, adding drag, particles, and sounds to sell the effect. Disabled by default.

**Features:**
* **Passable Foliage**: Leaves no longer block movement; entities can walk directly through them.
* **Leaf Drag**: Applies movement drag while inside leaves, scaling between a base and max value.
* **Leaf Rustle Sounds**: Plays rustling and breaking sound effects when moving through leaves.
* **Pale Oak Leaves Particles**: Spawns flung leaf particles when moving through Pale Oak leaves. Requires Vanilla Backport.
* **Tinted Leaves Particles**: Spawns flung leaf particles when moving through tinted leaves (Oak, Birch, Jungle, etc.). Requires Vanilla Backport.
* **Tinted Needles Particles**: Spawns flung needle particles when moving through Spruce leaves. Requires Vanilla Backport.
* **Cherry Leaves Particles**: Spawns flung leaf particles when moving through Cherry leaves.
