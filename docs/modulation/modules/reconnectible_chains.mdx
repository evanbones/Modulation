This module introduces brand new magical tools to make building with chains an absolute breeze.

**New Items & Blocks:**
* **Cast Post**: A specialized post block that can be placed in any direction and waterlogged.
* **Chain Staff**: A magical tool used to seamlessly place chains and posts at a distance! Hold right-click to charge it up and cast a block from afar.

**Usage Tips:**
* You can swap between **Chain Staff** modes by holding **Shift + Right-Clicking** with the item in your hand! In **Zipline** mode, you can use it to ride Ziplines from the mod [Ziplines Rezipped!](https://modrinth.com/mod/ziplines-rezipped)
