The Vanilla module focuses on fixing bugs and adding minor tweaks changes to the base game.

**Features:**
* **Fix Focus Bug (MC-259387)**: Fixes an annoying bug where buttons and sliders retain focus in GUIs.
* **Attack Sleeping Villagers (MC-148559)**: Ever tried to smack a villager who stole your bed, only to find out they're apparently unkillable? This tweak allows you to attack villagers while they are sleeping by modifying their hit radius.
* **Fix Dimension Experience Loss (MC-124177)**: Fixes a vanilla bug where experience levels disappear when changing dimensions without portals (e.g. when teleporting between worlds).
* **Fix Resource Filter Leak (MC-271761)**: Properly couples namespace and path checks for resource filters in `pack.mcmeta` to prevent them from leaking into other namespaces.
* **Fix Density Function Memoization (MC-268145)**: Fixes an exponential performance bug in Minecraft's world generation math. Most notably, this causes a major worldgen slowdown with mods like Tectonic.
* **Remove Anvil Limit**: Removes the 'Too Expensive!' limit for anvils, allowing you to perform operations regardless of the level cost.
* **No Anvil Enchant Cost**: Sets the experience cost for combining enchantments (via books or items) to 0.
* **No Anvil Repair Cost**: Sets the experience cost for all anvil repairs (using materials or combining damaged items) to 0.
* **No Anvil Rename Cost**: Sets the experience cost for all anvil renames to 0.
* **Nicer Map Colors**: Adjusts the vanilla map colors to use softer, more aesthetically pleasing palettes.
* **Ctrl+Click/Drag to Crafting Grid**: Hold Control while clicking or dragging items in your inventory to transfer them directly into the crafting grid.
* **Waxed Item Icon Overlay**: Displays a honeycomb icon on waxed items.
* **Extra Item Icon Overlays**: Displays redstone/silverfish icons on trapped chests and infested blocks.
* **Better Copper Tooltips**: Improves formatting and consistency of weathering/waxed copper tooltips.
