title: Block Grid & Surface Offsets
hide_meta: true
---

This module provides sub-block grid snapping (1/16th grid) and surface offset alignment for decorations, redstone components, and hanging entities.

**Features:**
* **Slab & Stair Surface Offsets**: Place blocks like lanterns, torches, flower pots, redstone wire, repeaters, and comparators on top of slabs and stairs.
* **Sign Surface Snapping**: Signs click-snap to exact 1/16th grid offsets.
* **Hanging Entities**: Item frames and paintings snap to sub-block surfaces.
* **Redstone Conduction**: Offset redstone components conduct signals properly.
* **Particle & Render Offsets**: Block particles and block entity rendering translates automatically to match block offsets.

All sub-features can be toggled individually in the configuration.
