### Added

- Added a ton of tweaks adapted from Forgery, and some more unique ones!
    - Added Better Pause Freezing tweak to freeze animated textures when paused in singleplayer.
    - Added Colored Crack Particles tweak to make item breaking particles inherit dye tints.
    - Added Ghast Charging Animation tweak restoring the classic charging animation.
    - Added Legible Signs tweak to adjust sign text contrast against wood.
    - Added Campfires Set Entities On Fire tweak.
    - Added Disable Axe Stripping tweak.
    - Added Disable Copper Scraping tweak.
    - Added No Dinnerlava tweak to disable single-block lava pockets in the Nether.
    - Added Ender Pearl Sound tweak to play teleport sounds on impact.
    - Added Spawn Egg Cracking particles and sound tweak.
    - Added Disable Anvil Damage tweak to prevent anvils from taking damage or breaking.
    - Added Loyalty tridents returning from the void).
    - Added Cactus Punching Hurts tweak.

    - Added Bedrock-like Impaling damage on wet entities tweak.

    - Added Fire Aspect behaves as flint & steel tweak.
