### Added

- Added Less Annoying Fire tweak to lower fire screen overlay and hide it with Fire Resistance or fire immunity.
- Added Pixel Consistent Block Drops tweak to render dropped 3D block items with Survival Test-style pixel scale.
- Added Dispenser Shears Pumpkins tweak to allow dispensers with shears to carve pumpkins.
- Added Water Bottles On Concrete Powder tweak to solidify concrete powder into concrete using water bottles or
  dispensers.
- Added Allow Mobs to Cross Rails tweak to fix MC-33359 where mobs refuse to pathfind across railway tracks.
