**Modulation** is the home for all of my small features that aren't big enough to warrant having their own mods.
It's a growing collection of quality-of-life tweaks, bug fixes, and cross-mod integrations for modpack creators.

From fixing annoying vanilla quirks to adding new features to your favorite mods like
[**Figura**](https://modrinth.com/mod/figura) or
[**Reconnectible Chains**](https://modrinth.com/mod/reconnectible-chains), Modulation has a module for you!

It's completely modular as well: turn off anything you don't need, and tweak as you like.

### Check out the Wiki!

For a full breakdown of every feature, how to configure the mod, and what each module does, please visit our
**[Wiki](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/modulation/latest/docs)**!
